about=Calculates SCIMAP spatial indices including network connectivity, erosion risk, in-channel risk, and flood mean/stdev outputs using native WhiteboxTools hydrological processing (BreachDepressions, FD8FlowAccumulation, D8Pointer, CostDistance) and a flow-path trace connectivity approach. The plugin includes standalone SCIMAP Network Index from DEM and SCIMAP Flood tools. Main SCIMAP workflow inputs are DEM, Land Cover Risk Weighting, and Rainfall rasters; the Flood workflow operates on pre-computed connectivity, runoff, rainfall, and overland flow distance rasters. Outputs include erosion, connectivity, in-channel risk rasters, a vector stream network, flood mean/stdev rasters, and an overland flow travel-distance raster. Depends upon the WhiteboxTools executable also being installed. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2010.08.022

about[es]=Calcula indices espaciales SCIMAP, incluida la conectividad de red, el riesgo de erosion, el riesgo en cauce y las salidas de media/desviacion estandar de inundacion, mediante el procesamiento hidrologico nativo de WhiteboxTools (BreachDepressions, FD8FlowAccumulation, D8Pointer) y un enfoque de conectividad por trazado de flujo.
